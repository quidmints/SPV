# SPV

QU!D — a dated, fully-backed dollar claim, issued against a diversified stablecoin basket, with
single-sided ETH and native-Bitcoin liquidity provision on the volatile side.

**Read [`spec.md`](./spec.md).** It is the protocol specification and it is the only document that
describes what this is.

Two other files are canonical for what they cover, and neither duplicates the spec:

- [`CLAUDE.md`](./CLAUDE.md) — how to work in this tree, and what the build environment actually is.
- [`docs/actionable/QUEUE.md`](./docs/actionable/QUEUE.md) — current status of open work.

⚠️ `docs/informational/` is prose that has drifted from the contracts in about ten places. Never
quote it without checking the code; `spec.md` §A is the standing ledger of those drifts.
